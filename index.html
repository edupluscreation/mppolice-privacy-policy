Edu Plus Creation ("we", "our", or "us") respects the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application MP Police 2025: Mock Test Prep (package: com.edupluscreation.mppolice).

2. Information We Collect (डेटा संग्रह)

We believe in minimal data collection. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver self-paced test practice and manage VIP pass access:

Data Type Collection Purpose Storage & Encryption
Mobile Phone Number User identification and unlocking 50 Full Mock Tests (VIP Pass). No password or OTP required. Encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and securely stored in PostgreSQL (Supabase).
Candidate Name (Optional) Personalizing test result scorecards and profile display. Stored securely in Supabase / Local Device Storage.
Test Practice Statistics Calculating accuracy percentage, weak topics (Mistake Bank), and mock scores. Stored locally on user's device (100% offline-ready).

4. Account and Data Deletion Policy (Google Play 2026 Compliance)

🗑️ Right to Delete Data (खाता और डेटा हटाने का अधिकार)

In full compliance with Google Play Store User Data and Account Deletion policies, any student can request complete deletion of their account and data at any time:

  1. In-App Deletion: Open the app → go to "प्रोफाइल (Profile)" → tap on "अकाउंट व डेटा हटाएं (Delete Account)" → Confirm. Your mobile number and profile will be permanently deleted from our servers immediately.
  2. Email Request: Send an email from your email address to edupluscreation@gmail.com with the subject "Request Account Deletion" and mention your registered 10-digit mobile number. We will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 24 to 48 hours and send you a confirmation.
